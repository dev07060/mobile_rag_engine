# Move-out checklist (apartment 4B)

- Give written notice to the landlord at least 60 days before the move-out date.
- Patch nail holes with filler; small holes are not charged, but large anchors are.
- Clean the oven and the fridge, including the shelves. The landlord deducts a cleaning fee of 120 dollars if they are left dirty.
- Return all three keys and both garage remotes.
- The security deposit is returned within 21 days, minus any deductions, to the bank account on the lease.
