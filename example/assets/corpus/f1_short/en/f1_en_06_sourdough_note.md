# Note to self: sourdough starter

Feed the starter once a day if it lives on the counter, or once a week if it is in the fridge.

Feeding ratio that works for me: 1 part starter, 1 part flour, 1 part water by weight.

It is ready to bake with when it has doubled within six hours and a spoonful floats in water.

If a grey liquid forms on top, it is just hungry. Pour it off and feed it. Pink or orange streaks mean it has spoiled and must be thrown away.
