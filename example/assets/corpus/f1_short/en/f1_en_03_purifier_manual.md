# AeroPure 300 Air Purifier: Filter Care

## Pre-filter
The washable pre-filter catches hair and large dust. Vacuum it or rinse it under cold water every two weeks, and let it dry completely before putting it back.

## HEPA filter
The HEPA filter cannot be washed. Replace it every six to eight months. When the filter indicator turns red, hold the Reset button for five seconds after installing the new filter.

## Odor
A burning smell during the first hours of use is normal and comes from the new carbon layer. If the smell lasts more than three days, contact support.
