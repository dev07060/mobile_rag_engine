# IT Helpdesk: VPN Connection Problems

## The client says "Authentication failed"
Your password expires every 90 days. Change it on the account portal first, then sign in to the VPN client with the new password.

## Connected, but internal sites do not load
Disconnect, open the client settings, and switch the protocol from Automatic to TCP. Hotel and airport networks often block the default UDP port.

## Still stuck?
Collect the log file from Help > Export Logs and attach it to a ticket. Phone support is available on weekdays from 8:00 to 18:00.
