# Pulse Tracker FAQ: Battery and Sync

## Why does the app use so much battery overnight?
Pulse Tracker keeps the heart-rate sensor awake while sleep tracking is on. If you do not need sleep data, turn off Sleep Tracking under Settings > Tracking and the overnight drain drops to under 3 percent.

## How often does my data sync?
Readings are uploaded every 15 minutes when the phone is on Wi-Fi. On mobile data the app waits until you open it, unless you enable Sync on Cellular.

## My watch shows "Paired" but no readings arrive.
Remove the watch from the Bluetooth list of the phone, restart both devices, and pair again from inside the app rather than from the system settings.
