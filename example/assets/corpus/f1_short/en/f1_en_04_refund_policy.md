# Northwind Goods Return and Refund Policy

You may return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

Opened software, gift cards, and personalized products cannot be returned.

Once the warehouse receives your parcel, the refund is issued to the original payment method within five business days. Card issuers may take another week to show the credit on your statement.

Return shipping is free for defective items. For all other returns, a flat fee of 6 dollars is deducted from the refund.
