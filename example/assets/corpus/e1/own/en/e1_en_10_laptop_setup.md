# New laptop setup (IT)

Before you start work on a new laptop:

1. Turn on full-disk encryption (FileVault on Mac, BitLocker on Windows). IT checks this automatically and blocks access to email on unencrypted laptops after 48 hours.
2. Install the Gatekeep VPN client from the Self Service app. You need the VPN for the internal wiki and the build servers.
3. Use the company password manager for every work account. Do not save work passwords in the browser.
4. Lock your screen when you leave your desk. Screens lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.

Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to it-help within one hour.
