# Reporting a suspicious email

If an email asks you to sign in, open an attachment you did not expect, or pay an invoice urgently, treat it as phishing.

- Do not click links or open attachments in it.
- Use the Report button in the mail toolbar. If you cannot find it, forward the email as an attachment to security@.
- If you already entered your password, change it immediately and tell the security team within one hour. Acting fast matters more than being sure.

The security team sends a test phishing email about once a quarter. Reporting it counts in your favor; nobody is punished for clicking.
