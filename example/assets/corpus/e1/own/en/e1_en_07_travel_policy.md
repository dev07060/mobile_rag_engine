# Travel and expense policy (internal)

Applies to all employees traveling for work.

- Book flights through the travel desk. Economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights.
- Meals are covered by a daily allowance of $60. You do not need receipts for meals within the allowance.
- Keep receipts for any other expense over $25.
- Submit the expense report within 30 days of returning. Reports submitted later need written approval from your director.
- Hotel bookings above $220 per night need approval from your manager before booking.
