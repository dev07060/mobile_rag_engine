# Lumo Home — Frequently Asked Questions

## How many devices can one hub control?
A single Lumo Hub supports up to 50 devices. If you need more, add a second hub and link it to the same home in the app under Home Settings > Hubs.

## Do my lights still work when the internet is down?
Yes. Switches, schedules and scenes run on the hub itself, so they keep working during an internet outage. Remote control from outside the house and voice assistants need the internet.

## Which voice assistants are supported?
Lumo works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. Apple Home is supported through Matter on hub firmware 3.1 or later.

## How do I add a new bulb?
Screw in the bulb, switch it on, then tap the + button on the Devices tab and choose Bulb. Keep the phone within 3 meters of the bulb until pairing finishes.

## How do I factory-reset the hub?
Hold the button on the back of the hub for 10 seconds until the ring light turns amber. All devices, scenes and schedules are removed from the hub.
