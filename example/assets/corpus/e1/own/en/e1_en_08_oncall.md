# On-call handbook (platform team)

The on-call rotation changes every Monday at 10:00. The person coming off call writes a short handover note in the #oncall channel.

When you are paged, acknowledge within 15 minutes. If you cannot, the page escalates to the secondary and then to the engineering manager.

Severity 1 incidents (the product is down for all customers) need a status page update within 30 minutes.

If you were paged between midnight and 6:00, you may take the next morning off. A full week of on-call earns one extra day of leave.
