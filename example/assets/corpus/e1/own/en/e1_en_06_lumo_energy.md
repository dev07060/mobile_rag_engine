# Energy reports

The Energy tab estimates how much electricity your lights and smart plugs use. Plugs measure power directly. For bulbs, Lumo multiplies the bulb's rated wattage by the time it was on at each brightness level, so bulb figures can be off by about 10 percent.

A monthly summary is emailed on the first day of each month. Turn it off under Profile > Notifications > Monthly energy email.

To see cost instead of kWh, enter your electricity price per kWh under Energy > Settings > Tariff.
