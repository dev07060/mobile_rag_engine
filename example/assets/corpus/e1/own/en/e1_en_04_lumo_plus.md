# Lumo Plus subscription

Lumo Plus costs $3.99 per month or $39 per year and covers every home on your account.

What you get:
- 30 days of camera clip history (without Plus, clips are kept for 24 hours).
- Away Mode, which turns lights on and off in a natural pattern while you travel.
- Priority chat support.

Your first month is free. Cancel the subscription in the App Store or Google Play, not in the Lumo app; access continues until the end of the paid period. Clips older than 24 hours are deleted 7 days after Plus ends.
