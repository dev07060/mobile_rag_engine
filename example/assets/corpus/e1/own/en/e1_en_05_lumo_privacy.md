# Lumo privacy summary

Camera video is processed on the hub for motion and person detection. Video leaves your home only when you save a clip or view a camera remotely.

We collect device names, on/off events and app crash reports to run the service. We do not sell personal data.

To delete your account, open Profile > Privacy > Delete account. The account is disabled at once and all data, including saved clips, is erased within 30 days. Backups that contain your data expire after another 35 days.
