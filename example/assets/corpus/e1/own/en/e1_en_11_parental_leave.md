# Parental leave policy

All employees with at least six months of service get 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, regardless of gender or how the child joins the family.

- Tell your manager and People Ops at least 8 weeks before the planned start, when possible.
- Leave can be taken in up to two blocks within the first 12 months.
- During the first two weeks back you may work 60 percent of your normal hours at full pay.
