# Lumo Home 4.2 Release Notes

Released March 3.

## New
- Sunrise Wake: bedroom lights fade in over 15, 30 or 45 minutes before your alarm. Find it under Routines > New routine > Sunrise Wake.
- Room widgets for the home screen on iOS and Android.

## Fixed
- Schedules no longer skip one run on the night clocks change for daylight saving time.
- The app no longer shows a hub as offline right after the phone wakes from sleep.

## Changed
- Android 9 is no longer supported. The minimum is now Android 10. Version 4.1 keeps working on Android 9 but will not receive updates.
